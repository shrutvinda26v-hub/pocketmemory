She is watching.

A jeweled ewe of uncertain origin. She chews a daisy, blinks when she likes, and her eyes follow the pointer around the page.

Field notes

What we know so far

01

The gaze

Pupils track left, right, up, and down with a soft ease. Leave the window and she looks forward again. She blinks every few seconds, sometimes twice.

02

The daisy

The flower in her mouth is from the original study reel. She works it the way sheep do — a small, stubborn chew, never quite finished.

03

The jewels

Crown, collar, and drop earrings stay put. Fur, lighting, and the sky behind her are still. Only the eyes and the daisy are alive.

The keep

Not a zoo. A sitting room.

Visiting hours are whenever you open the page. Speak softly. Do not offer a second daisy until she has finished the first.
